# The fractal-governance movement - timeline

Adapted 2026-09-25 from `ZAOfractal/reference/14-timeline.md` (`ZAODEVZ/ZAOfractal`,
`~/Desktop/repos/ZAOfractal`), whose pre-2024 entries were independently verified 2026-05-21
against primary sources (Amazon, Medium, edenfractal.com, hive.blog) and whose 2024+ entries are
sourced from ZAO's internal research library and on-chain data. This file trims that source's
scope to the movement's public milestones and ZAO's place in it; for ZAO Fractal's own detailed
history, see `zao-fractal-timeline.md` in this directory - it is not repeated here past a
handoff point.

## What this deliberately leaves out

Per the source document: Roy Fractal, Fractal Hispano, Alien Worlds Fractal, Aquadac, EOS
Respect, ZEOS Fractal - all founded around 2022 in Fractally's wake, running on their own
schedules, not milestone-dated here. See `ZAOfractal/reference/10-fractal-communities-
directory.md` if a future agent needs them. Also left out deliberately: internal Optimystics repo
commits, ZAO research docs, and individual session-by-session ZAO Fractal events - this file
tracks public, external-facing milestones for the movement as a whole, not ZAO's internal
history, which is `zao-fractal-timeline.md`'s job.

## Where the two files hand off

This file's source stops at 2026-05-21 (Doc 703). Everything ZAO-specific from that point
forward - the bot rebuild, Season 3, the September 2026 governance restructuring - lives in
`zao-fractal-timeline.md`, not duplicated here.
